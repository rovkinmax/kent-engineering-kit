# Objective

Prepare successors for `$ARGUMENTS` on an admitted workflow, preserving useful
work, original authority and restrictions. Preparation ends at an exact
preview; creation, transfer, launch, cancellation and cleanup are separate
effects requiring applicable approval.

# Discover

Inspect each predecessor and continuation chain with supported read-only
interfaces. Resolve current workflow/source/storage bindings, not assumed
latest versions. Record:

- outcome, exclusions, original decisions, unanswered questions and supersession;
- accepted/working plans, completed work and remaining acceptance;
- source baseline, HEAD, index/worktree delta and untracked product files,
  separating generated/private artifacts;
- existing successors and accepted results, avoiding duplicate completed work;
- source/environment receipts, stale proof and unknown effects;
- consumed attempts, resource owners, cleanup duties and technical limits;
- dependencies and closure effects; sibling evidence only when relevant.

Use explicit transfer, historical-context, deliberately-omit and unknown
dispositions. No live Session does not prove released resources. Missing
evidence is unknown, not inapplicable or consent.

# Consolidate

Select a target supporting remaining work, including no-diff diagnosis and
negative results where needed. Keep the immutable comparison baseline separate
from the integration target. Do not copy obsolete helpers/storage or embedded
old Task identities into new work by default; preserve historical receipts.

Check initial transfer, normal continuation and recovery/revalidation together:
edge outputs, producer/consumer prompts, guards, locked instructions and pending
approval snapshots must agree. Report infrastructure mismatch through its own
capable recovery owner, not fabricated product Fix.

Write a readable successor body: goal, completed work, remaining work and
acceptance. Link durable technical evidence and original decisions; retain
material restrictions directly in the body. Identify exact transferred artifacts,
deliberate loss and unknown recoverability. New identity restores no attempts
and transfers no resource ownership. Name how writers will be serialized and
who retains predecessor cleanup.

# Preview and decision

Present exact successor bodies, workflow/source bindings, transfer boundaries,
dependency updates, predecessor disposition, owners, verification and rollback.
List intended effects independently: creation, initialization, transfer,
execution, cancellation, publication and cleanup. Reuse applicable verified
decisions; obtain only missing scope/effect authority. Follow required
independent governance review for Kit/workflow changes.

Choose a compatible workflow before creation. If a safe transfer or continuation
route is missing, keep that successor prepared/not-created; do not assume Start
can change its workflow.

# Execute approved effects

Resolve supported transfer stage/owner and task-fixed storage first. Launch may
automatically reach Implement: do not treat it as idle worktree materialization,
race Start/Interrupt, seed alongside a writer or invent prestart helpers.
Initialize through qualified project admission. Preserve predecessor roots,
history and admitted infrastructure.

Before dependency-sensitive execution, verify links or an equivalent supported
blocking mechanism. Recheck current preimages and use one predecessor/successor
mapping. Unknown creation/effect outcomes require lookup/settlement before
retry or dependent effects. Continue independent work where safe.

Verify transferred source and context and the actual next continuation route.
Do not exercise an unauthorized product transition to prove routing. A rejected
completion requires bounded diagnosis and supported recovery, not unchanged
replay. Reverify only checks whose bindings/next gate require it; do not relabel
old receipts or rerun everything merely because identity changed.

# Completion

Distinguish prepared, created, transfer-verified, instructions-loaded,
plan-accepted, executable and blocked. Retain rollback and cleanup obligations.
Close a predecessor only after verified transfer, preserved restrictions and
dependency links, settled or safely retained resources, and explicit
cancellation authority. Successor product acceptance is not an extra migration
prerequisite absent a real dependency.

At the agreed endpoint, stop only migration-owned observers and verify they
ended. Report retained duties; migration does not become ongoing product
supervision. Rollback stops further effects and preserves verified state; it
does not authorize deleting Tasks, branches or history.
